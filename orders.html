Simple Process

How to Place a Custom Order

From first inquiry to final bite — here's how it works.

1

Submit Your Inquiry

Fill out our order form with your event date, guest count, theme inspiration, and flavor preferences. We'll get back to you within 24 hours.

2

Design Consultation

We schedule a call or in-studio visit to discuss your vision in detail. Our team creates hand-sketched concepts and flavor combinations for your review.

3

Tasting Session

Visit our Brooklyn studio to sample your chosen flavors. Our tasting box includes 6 curated samples to help you finalize your perfect profile.

4

Confirm & Pay Deposit

Review the final design sketch and quote. Secure your date with a 50% deposit. You'll receive a detailed order confirmation via email.

5

Creation & Delivery

Our team handcrafts your dessert. We handle temperature-controlled delivery and optional on-site setup so you can focus on your celebration.

Enjoy!

Celebrate with confidence. Your dessert is freshly baked, beautifully presented, and ready to make your event unforgettable.
